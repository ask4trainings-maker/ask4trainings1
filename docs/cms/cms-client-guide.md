# Hope InfoTech — Client Content Management Guide

Welcome to the **Hope InfoTech Content Management System (CMS)**! This guide is designed for content managers, editors, and administrators to help you create, update, and publish content on the Hope InfoTech website without writing any code.

---

## 1. Accessing the CMS

To access the CMS for the live website:

1. Open your web browser and navigate to **`https://your-domain.com/admin/`** (or your live site URL followed by `/admin/`).
2. Click **"Log in with GitHub"** (or use your credentials).
3. Once authenticated, you will see the CMS Dashboard.

---

## 2. Navigating the CMS Dashboard

On the left sidebar, you will find two main types of content:

### **Collections (Items you can add many of)**
- **Courses:** Manage all IT training course pages (e.g., Guidewire, Siemens Teamcenter, DevOps, SAP).
- **Blog & Guides:** Manage articles, news, and learning guides.

### **Settings & Static Pages (Singletons / One-off pages)**
- **Site Settings & Nav:** Update top bar phone numbers, email, navigation menus, and footer text.
- **Homepage Content:** Update hero titles, subtitles, statistics counters (students trained, placement rate), and category grids.
- **About / Services / Contact Pages:** Update office addresses, batch timings, map links, and corporate training details.

---

## 3. The Lifecycle of a Change: Drafts to Live Website

The CMS uses an **Editorial Workflow** to protect your live site. This means **nothing goes live until you explicitly click "Publish"**.

```
[ 📝 Save Draft ]  ➔  [ 👁️ In Review ]  ➔  [ ✅ Ready ]  ➔  [ 🚀 Publish ]
```

### Stage 1: Creating or Editing a Draft
- When you create a new course/article or edit an existing one, click the **"Save"** button at the top right.
- This creates a **Draft**. 
- **Drafts are invisible to the public.** They are safely stored in the background.

### Stage 2: Reviewing Your Work
- At the top of your editing screen, you will see a status badge (**Draft**).
- You can change the status from **Draft** to **In Review** or **Ready** when you finish writing.
- You can view all active drafts at any time by clicking the **"Workflow"** tab at the top of the CMS.

### Stage 3: Publishing to the Live Website
- Once an item's status is set to **Ready**, a green **"Publish"** button will appear in the top header.
- When you click **"Publish"**:
  1. The CMS sends the changes to the website's publishing system.
  2. The website automatically builds and deploys your updates in **1 to 2 minutes**.
  3. Your updates will be live for all visitors!

## 4. How to Update Specific Content

### A. Updating Course Details & Fees
1. Click **Courses** in the left sidebar.
2. Click on the course you want to update (e.g., *Guidewire PolicyCenter Training*).
3. Update any of the fields:
   - **Upcoming Live Batch Details:** Change the Batch Start Date, Class Timings, or Seats Left tag.
   - **Pricing Tiers:** Change course fees, plan names, or included features.
   - **Curriculum:** Add, remove, or re-order course modules and topics.
   - **Trainer Profile:** Update instructor name, experience, and avatar photo.
4. Click **Save** to keep as a draft, or change status to **Ready** and click **Publish** to send it live.

### B. Adding a New Blog Post
1. Click **Blog & Guides** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **"New Blog & Guides"** button.
3. Fill in the **Title**, **Author**, **Publish Date**, **Cover Image**, and **Body** (using the rich-text markdown editor).
4. Click **Save** ➔ Set to **Ready** ➔ Click **Publish**.

### C. Updating Contact Numbers or Office Address
1. Click **Site Settings & Nav** in the left sidebar.
2. Select **Navigation & Menus**.
3. Under **Top Bar Contacts**, update primary/secondary phone numbers or email.
4. Click **Save** and **Publish**.

---

## 5. Accessibility Guidelines for Images

Whenever you upload an image (course banners, trainer photos, or blog covers):
- **Always fill in the "Image Alt Text" field.**
- Provide a clear, short description of the image (e.g., `"Guidewire PolicyCenter Classroom Session in Ameerpet"`).
- This ensures your site remains accessible for visual impairments and ranks higher on Google SEO.

## Quick Reference Summary

| Action | What to click | Who sees it? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Save progress without publishing | **Save** | Only you (inside CMS) |
| Mark ready for launch | Set Status to **Ready** | Team / Reviewers |
| Push changes live to public site | **Publish** | Everyone on the internet (~2 mins) |
