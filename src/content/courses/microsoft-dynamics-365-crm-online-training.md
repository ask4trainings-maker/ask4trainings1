## Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM Online Training

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement (CRM) is a cloud-first, enterprise-grade platform that unifies Sales, Customer Service, Marketing, and Field Service into a single, integrated system built on Microsoft Dataverse and the Power Platform.

Organisations worldwide use Dynamics 365 CRM to manage customer relationships, automate business processes, gain AI-driven insights, and deliver personalised customer experiences at scale.

### What You Will Learn

This programme covers both **functional** and **technical** dimensions of the platform:

- **Functional Track**: Sales pipeline management, customer service case resolution, SLA configuration, business process flows, and marketing automation.
- **Technical Track**: JavaScript client-side customisations, C# plugin development, Web API integration, Power Platform connectivity, and Azure service integration.

### Core Business Modules Covered

| Module | Key Capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Sales** | Leads, opportunities, quotes, orders, products, goals |
| **Customer Service** | Cases, queues, SLAs, entitlements, knowledge base |
| **Marketing** | Customer journeys, segments, email marketing, lead scoring |
| **Power Platform** | Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, Dataverse |

### Career Opportunities

Dynamics 365 CRM professionals are among the most in-demand enterprise technology specialists. After completing this training, you will be positioned for roles including:

- **Dynamics 365 CRM Functional Consultant** — implementing and configuring CE modules for enterprise clients
- **Dynamics 365 Technical Developer** — building plugins, PCF controls, and API integrations
- **Power Platform Developer** — extending CRM with canvas apps, flows, and dashboards
- **Solution Architect** — designing end-to-end Dynamics 365 architectures

### Why Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM?

- **Global Adoption**: Used by 40,000+ organisations in 150+ countries
- **Microsoft Ecosystem Integration**: Deep native connectivity with Teams, Outlook, Azure, and Power Platform
- **AI-First Design**: Embedded Copilot and AI capabilities across every module
- **Career Growth**: High-paying roles with consistently growing demand across North America, UK, and the Middle East
