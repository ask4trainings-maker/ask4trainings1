# Shipping Policy

Ask4Trainings provides digital educational services and online training. Therefore, we do not ship physical products.

## 1. Digital Delivery
All course materials, including lecture access, PDFs, assignments, and software lab credentials, are delivered electronically.

## 2. Delivery Timeline
Upon successful enrollment and payment confirmation, you will receive your LMS login credentials and onboarding instructions via email within 24 hours.

## 3. Account Access
Your digital access is tied to the email address provided during registration. Please ensure your contact details are accurate to avoid delays in receiving your course materials.
