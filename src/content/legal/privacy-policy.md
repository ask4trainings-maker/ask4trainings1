# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how Ask4Trainings collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

## 1. Student Data Handling
We collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and educational background when you enroll in our courses. This data is used exclusively to facilitate your learning experience, grant access to our LMS, and provide course updates.

## 2. Lead Inquiry Storage
Information provided through contact forms, inquiries, or demo session registrations is securely stored. We use this information to respond to your queries and inform you about relevant upcoming batches. We do not sell or rent your information to third-party marketers.

## 3. Payment Gateway Data Standards
All financial transactions are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways. Ask4Trainings does not store your credit card details or sensitive financial data on our servers.
